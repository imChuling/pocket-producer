Step 1

Read the session

Pocket Producer reads the open Audiotool document through Nexus and builds a session fingerprint. It shows exactly what it read.

Audiotool documents carry no key signature, so the field says "not available" rather than guessing. The musician adds intent in their own words.

Import your back catalog

Each sample goes through the standard ingest pipeline: CLAP embedding, tagging, storage. After import, those fragments rank alongside everything else.

Innovation

Honest uncertainty as a design principle

Model is selectable
Rules baseline is the default. Learned fusion is opt-in because its advantage depends on label construction.
Evidence, not scores
Every suggestion carries per-signal evidence drawn from the ranker's actual features. Never generated prose.
Graceful fallback
If fusion is unavailable, rules baseline answers automatically. The badge tells you which model served.
Missing = "not available"
Session data the document doesn't carry is reported honestly. Never imputed, never guessed.
Research

A measured system

Submitted to ISMIR 2026 as a Late-Breaking/Demo paper. Six baselines, frozen held-out split, sensitivity analysis.

The learned fusion shows a 7.8 pp sensitivity on the hardest examples across audio embedding and training configurations. This is why the fusion is opt-in.
