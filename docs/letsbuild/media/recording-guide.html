Demo Recording Guide

配音稿（口语版英文）+ 逐镜头录制指南。目标时长 4:15–4:45，上限 5:00。

配音稿和 deck 里按 N 键显示的备注是同一套文本。每个镜头分三块：🎙 说什么、🖱 屏幕上做什么、⚠ 注意事项。

0 · 录制前准备

  • 浏览器开新窗口（非隐身，需要已登录状态），关掉所有多余标签页、书签栏、插件图标
  • 窗口设为 1920×1080 或用系统全屏；系统通知开勿扰模式
  • Audiotool 里提前准备一个半成品项目：4 轨左右（鼓 + synth），120 BPM，有明显「缺 bass」的空缺，播放头停在一个好听的位置
  • fragment 库里确认有 3 个以上 bass 类素材，且 rules 模型能把它们排上来（提前跑一遍确认推荐质量）
  • 先把整个流程完整走一遍不录音，确认没有加载报错、fallback badge 意外出现
  • 录屏工具：QuickTime / OBS，30fps 即可；系统声音和麦克风分轨录（方便后期）
  • 配音建议后期配：先录屏幕（按下面的动作脚本操作，动作间留 1–2 秒静止），再照稿配音，最后对齐
⚠ 全程不要出现:个人邮箱以外的账号信息、浏览器历史、其他项目名。OAuth 弹窗里的内容是可以出现的(这正是要展示的)。

2 · 问题 0:15 – 0:40

Shot 2 · 半成品项目 + 素材文件夹deck 第 2 页 或 实拍
🎙 Voiceover Here's the moment this tool is built for. You open a track you left half-finished last week. You've got dozens of your own loops sitting in a folder somewhere -- but no way to ask: which of these actually belongs with what's already here? Most tools answer with similarity -- "what sounds like this." But that's the wrong question. We call the right question session compatibility.
🖱 Screen
  1. Audiotool 打开那个半成品项目,按空格播放 3–4 秒,再停下
  2. 切到素材文件夹/library 页面,慢慢滚动一屏,表现「很多素材、无从下手」
💡 播放的那几秒是全片唯一的「project 原声」,选个好听的 loop 段落,后期压低音量垫在配音下面。

3 · 产品导览：一库四面 0:40 – 1:35

Shot T1 · Capture：四种输入deck 第 3–4 页
🎙 Voiceover Pocket Producer is four surfaces around one library. Capture is the front door, and there are four ways in: tap to record, type a lyric, drop an audio file -- or pull a whole Audiotool project in as one mixed-down fragment. Every input becomes a fragment, and the agent pipeline tags it: instruments, emotions, themes, plus one concrete suggestion. The cards are a working notebook -- rename, reanalyze, notes, timestamped comments.
🖱 Screen
  1. 打开 pocketproducer.vercel.app，左栏从上到下慢扫：录音钮 → 文本框 → dropzone → "Import from Audiotool"
  2. 在文本框实际打字一句歌词点发送，"Saved!" 出现，新卡进入 Processing
  3. 等 tagging 完成（约 15s，可后期剪短），鼠标划过 tags 和 AI suggestion
  4. 划过一张 Audiotool 导入的卡（暖橙色调）和一张普通 audio 卡（蓝色调）形成对比
💡 Audiotool 混合导入的完整过程放在后面 Shot 4.5，这里只露按钮和已导入的卡片，避免重复。
Shot T2 · Projects：自动分组 + rescue scoredeck 第 5 页
🎙 Voiceover Here's the part you never do by hand: filing. Agents group related fragments into projects -- same song candidates, shared themes, similar emotion -- and every connection states its reason. Each project gets a rescue score, how alive it still is, and one concrete next action. Scan to re-group, reset to start over.
🖱 Screen
  1. 切到 Projects 页，顶部 "11 projects assembled from your fragments" 可见
  2. 第一张项目卡停 2 秒：分数 90、connection chips、connection reason
  3. 滚动划过 next action 文本，右上 Scan / Reset 按钮划过不点
Shot T3 · DNA：创作者画像deck 第 6 页
🎙 Voiceover DNA turns the same library inward: emotion radar, style bubbles, theme distribution, an hourly heatmap of when ideas actually happen, and a network view of how fragments connect. A creative profile computed from what you actually made -- not what you claim you make. That's the memory half. Now the instrument half: Audiotool.
🖱 Screen
  1. 切到 DNA 页，archetype 卡（The Poet + 情绪条）停 2 秒
  2. 匀速下滚：emotion flow → 雷达图 → style bubbles → 时间热力图，各停约 1.5 秒
  3. "Peak creative window 23:00–1:00" 划过（深夜创作是个好记忆点）
💡 这一段全程滚动即可，不需要点击，配音结束时正好停在页面底部。

4 · 连接 Audiotool 1:35 – 2:00

Shot 3 · OAuth 流程deck 第 9 页占位
🎙 Voiceover So let's see it for real. Over in Pocket Producer, I click Connect. Audiotool asks me to approve two scopes -- project write and sample write -- and importantly, that login stays right here in my browser. Our backend never sees the token. My projects show up, and I pick the one I've been stuck on.
🖱 Screen
  1. 打开 pocketproducer.vercel.app/audiotool,停 1 秒让面板看清
  2. 鼠标划过 "Your Audiotool login never leaves this browser" 那行字(不点)
  3. 点 Connect → OAuth 弹窗出现 → 停 1 秒让 scope 列表被看到 → 授权
  4. 项目列表出现 → 点目标项目
⚠ OAuth 弹窗出现到授权之间留足停顿,这是「真集成」的第一个证据帧,评审要看清 scope。

5 · Session fingerprint 2:00 – 2:30

Shot 4 · 读取会话 + 输入 intentdeck 第 11 页占位
🎙 Voiceover The first thing it does is read the session: tempo, the tracks, what roles they play, where my playhead is. And check out the Key field -- it says "not available." Audiotool documents don't carry a key signature, so we say so instead of guessing. Then there's one thing it can't read from the document: what I'm going for. So I just type it -- "dark bass to sit under these drums." And I'll pick how many suggestions I want -- nudge me with one, or let me explore six. I'll take three.
🖱 Screen
  1. fingerprint 面板填充出来后停 2 秒
  2. 鼠标依次划过 Tempo → Key ("not available") → Track types
  3. 在 intent 输入框实际打字(别粘贴):dark bass to sit under these drums
  4. intensity 选 Suggest (3);顺手让模型下拉露出一下 Rules / Fusion 两个选项再收起
💡 打字过程保留实速,真实感比流畅更重要。

5.5 · 导入旧工程（可选加分镜头） +15s

Shot 4.5 · Import samples to library新功能
🎙 Voiceover One more thing your library can do: pull from your own history. I open an older project and click "Import samples to library" -- every sample in it flows through the same ingest pipeline and becomes searchable creative memory. Your back catalog can now be recommended into new sessions.
🖱 Screen
  1. 回到项目列表,打开一个旧工程
  2. 点头部的 "Import samples to library",进度 "Importing 3/5..." 可见
  3. 成功提示 "Imported 5 of 5 samples into your library." 停 2 秒
💡 放在 Shot 4 之后最顺("库从哪来"的回答)。时间紧可剪掉,不影响主线。录之前先在别的工程试一次,确认 ingest 管线延迟正常。

6 · 排序与证据 2:30 – 3:10

Shot 5 · 候选 + evidence chips + 试听deck 第 13 页占位
🎙 Voiceover Now the part I care most about. Every suggestion comes with evidence -- real evidence from the ranker's own features, not generated text. This chip says the loop fills a role the session doesn't have yet -- there's no bass. This one says the tempo matches. This one, that it matches what I typed. If you can't see why a recommendation was made, you can't trust it. Let me preview the top one... yeah, that could work.
🖱 Screen
  1. 点 rank/suggest 按钮,候选卡出现
  2. 鼠标逐个划过第一张卡的 3 个 evidence chip,每个停约 1 秒,和配音对齐
  3. 点 Preview,让试听播 3–4 秒(这段声音保留进成片)
  4. 顺带划过 "Served by rules-v1" badge
⚠ chip 划动顺序要和配音顺序一致:role gap → tempo → intent。录屏时按这个顺序,配音就好对。

7 · 插入与撤销 3:10 – 3:50

Shot 6 · Insert → Audiotool 时间线 → Undodeck 第 15 页占位
🎙 Voiceover Insert is a single Nexus transaction: upload the sample, wait for the server to confirm, place it at the playhead. Switch over to Audiotool -- and there it is, on a new track, right at the playhead. Let's hear it in context... Now say I change my mind. Undo here isn't "delete the last thing" -- the insert produced a receipt of every entity it created, and undo restores the exact prior state. One click... and the project is exactly what it was. Nothing lost, nothing left behind.
🖱 Screen
  1. 点 "Insert into Audiotool",loading 状态露出一瞬
  2. 切到 Audiotool 标签页,新 track + 播放头处的 sample 清晰可见,停 2 秒
  3. 按空格播放 4–5 秒(bass 和鼓一起响,这是全片高光,声音保留)
  4. 切回 Pocket Producer,绿色成功条可见 → 点 Undo
  5. 再切回 Audiotool,轨道恢复原状,停 2 秒
💡 插入后的合奏播放是「musical value」的直接证据,这 5 秒别吝啬。undo 前后各停 2 秒形成对照。

8 · 创新与研究 3:50 – 4:20

Shot 7 · 设计原则 + ISMIR 一页deck 第 16–17 页
🎙 Voiceover One design choice worth explaining: the learned ranker is opt-in, not the default. We actually measured this system for ISMIR 2026 -- six baselines, a frozen held-out split -- and the learned model's advantage swings by nearly eight points depending on embedding and training choices alone. So the product tells the truth about that: transparent rules by default, the learned model one click away, and every score treated as a proposal, not a judgment.
🖱 Screen deck 第 16 页(四张原则卡)停 6 秒 → 第 17 页(sensitivity 表)停 8 秒。也可以在表格页把 "7.8 pp" 处轻微 zoom。
⚠ 口径:7.8pp 只说 "swings depending on configuration",不说 causes / proves。这和论文口径一致。
